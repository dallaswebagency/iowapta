Iowa PTA Updates

What is the updates section?

March 4, 2024

Hello PTA family,

It’s Maurice A Davis, your current IPTA president. I wanted to share something exciting with you through this post, as I believe in the power of face-to-face communication, even if it’s digital. My aim is to become a more familiar presence in your lives, showing up in various ways we connect, and today marks the beginning of that journey.

I’m thrilled to introduce a new feature on our website – the update section. This is our new communication channel, ensuring you’re in the loop with all things PTA. Whether there’s a change within our association or an update to our website, you’ll hear it first from this section.

For quite some time, our website wasn’t being updated.

But, with my digital background, I’m set on changing that. This website will become a reliable source for all the information you need.

However, it’s a vast space, and I need your keen eyes to help keep it current. If you stumble upon outdated information, snap a screenshot and send it my way. This will help me make swift updates, ensuring you have access to the latest details.

To make communication seamless, I’ll include a link at the end of this post. It’ll guide you to the best way to reach out without directly sharing my email here, to avoid any confusion should it change in the future.

Click Here for Contact Information

Your patience and cooperation mean the world to me. Together, we’re not just updating a website; we’re enhancing our PTA experience. You’ve been incredibly patient, and I assure you, improvements are on the horizon, even if they’re not immediately visible.

I’m eager to collaborate with you to make our website more user-friendly and informative. You’ll notice updates to our contact information, including a new phone number and address, ensuring you can always reach me when needed.

Thank you for all your support and for being an integral part of our PTA community. Let’s keep winning, together.

Warm regards,
