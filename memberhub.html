MemberHub

MemberHub will be providing several training sessions for all PTA officers/leaders, so please be sure to register ASAP using the links below. After registering, you will receive a confirmation email with instructions for joining the webinar.

  • Welcome to Memberhub: A Getting Started Overview

Monday, March 22, 2021 6:30pm

  • Memberhub for Communication and Compliance - Iowa PTA

Monday, March 29, 2021 6:30pm

  • Using Your Online Store and Fundraisers - Iowa PTA

Monday, May 5, 2021 6:30pm

Each session will include time for Q&A, so if you haven’t checked out the MemberHub Welcome Page for PTAs, please do so now at http://info.memberhub.com/pta.

We’ve teamed up with MemberHub to provide their valuable online tool to all PTAs in Iowa …free of charge! MemberHub makes it easier to run your PTA — engage families, raise funds, stay in good standing, and more. You’ll save hours on admin tasks, so you can focus on connecting with your children and engaging your school community.

MemberHub is valued at $1,200 per year, but they are providing it to Iowa PTAs for free because MemberHub and Iowa PTA want to further the PTA mission through easy-to-use technology.

As of the 2020-2021 school year, MemberHub will be used to manage members, make state dues payments, report officers, and record good standing requirements, but we also hope you take advantage of the many additional great features available through your free MemberHub site!

If you have questions about our state implementation of MemberHub, please contact us at info@iowapta.org or 319-382-0729.

Here are some of MemberHub’s most popular features:

  • Your Own Public Website and E-Store: Keep your school community in the know with your free public website and sell PTA memberships (including custom/bundled memberships), spirit wear, event tickets, collect donations, and more — all online in your own MemberHub store.
  • Communication Tools: Keep families in the loop with email and text capabilities, calendars, signups, file sharing, directories, and more — to everyone on your site or to smaller groups of people you can organize in hubs as you see fit — on your own private MemberHub site.
  • Fundraising: In addition to selling items and collecting donations in your store, MemberHub provides a simple way to raise funds through SponsorShare. Sponsors get advertising in emails you send through MemberHub and you get a no-fuss way to add funds to your budget! Click here to learn more about SponsorShare. Also click here to learn more about the Fundraising Campaigns feature and Givebacks for the win-win-win… both new for 2020!
  • Easily Stay in Good Standing: Your free site provides automatic member management, member reporting, dues remittance to the state, and visibility of state PTA compliance tasks.

PLEASE NOTE: We need your help! From now unitl the end of the 2020-2021 school year, we need the following done through MemberHub by all PTAs:

Verify and/or add officers for the current and next school year (if known) — REMINDER: President, VicePresident, Secretary, and Treasurer must be listed on your MemberHub site’s Officers page for your unit to remain in good standing (the rest of the titles are optional).

Add additional PTA leaders and your generic PTA email addresses (if desired) as “People” on your site and assign admin rights as you see fit.

Families and staff may be added to your site at any time (as People, as opposed to paid PTA Members), so you can start taking advantage of the communications features right away.
