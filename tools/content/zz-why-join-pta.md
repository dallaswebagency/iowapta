# Why Join PTA?

When you join PTA, you add your voice to more than 4,000 members and over 130 local units across Iowa, all working to make every child's potential a reality. Whether you're a parent, teacher, grandparent, student or community member, there's a place for you.

## What your membership does

**A voice for every child.** Iowa PTA is a grassroots advocate for the health, education and safety of children and youth. Members help shape our legislative platform and speak up at the Statehouse and in Washington, DC.

**Programs your school can use.** Members bring National PTA programs like Reflections, School of Excellence, Family Reading Experience, STEM + Families, Healthy Lifestyles and Connect for Respect to their schools and communities.

**Support for PTA leaders.** Local units get toolkits, eLearning, ready-to-use forms and free MemberHub tools for managing members, dues and communication, plus training at our annual convention and fall leadership conference.

**Scholarships for Iowa students.** Iowa PTA's H.L. Taylor Scholarship Program awards grants to deserving Iowa students every year.

**Member benefits and savings.** As a PTA member you also belong to National PTA and can take advantage of member offers and discounts.

**Your time, your way.** Volunteer time is never a requirement of membership. Join to show your support, help when you can, or step up to lead. Every member counts.

## Ways to join

[Join a local PTA unit](https://iowapta.org/join-pta/) Most members join through the PTA at their child's school. Contact your school's PTA or reach out to us and we'll help you find your unit.

[IPTA Neva Taylor Statewide Unit](https://ipta.memberhub.com/store/items/630676) No local school unit? Join statewide. All proceeds from this unit support the H.L. Taylor Scholarship Program, and you're also a member of National PTA.

[See PTA member benefits](https://www.pta.org/home/About-National-Parent-Teacher-Association/Benefits-of-PTA-Membership/Save-Money-with-Member-Offers) Discounts and offers available to every PTA member.

[Support Iowa PTA with a donation](https://www.memberplanet.com/campaign/iowapta/communitydonating_146) Iowa PTA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your contribution is tax-deductible.
